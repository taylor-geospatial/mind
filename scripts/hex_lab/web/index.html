What am I looking at?

At low zoom, resolution-3/4 cells show count-weighted overviews. Zoom in for the original resolution-5 cells. Empty areas have no cells in this file.

PCA uses a fixed 2nd–98th percentile stretch per channel. Contrast is the mean cosine distance to available neighboring resolution-5 cells, then averaged in overviews.

Similarity compares L2-normalized vectors. Distant matches always use the source resolution-5 cells. Gray contrast cells have no available neighbors.
